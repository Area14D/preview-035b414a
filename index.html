Getting here點樣嚟

Gee Wing Chang Industrial Building, 6 Fung Yip Street, Chai Wan. Cargo lift to 14/F.

Driving and loading
Stop free for a short time on the ground floor of Gee Wing Chang Industrial Building to load and unload. Cargo lift to 14/F.
Parking
First pick: Kailey Industrial Centre car park, 12 Fung Yip Street. The open-air Fung Yip Street Car Park on the same street also takes vans.
MTR and minibus
Chai Wan Exit C: green minibus 47M (towards Siu Sai Wan) from the minibus terminus, or the 20M circular from the bus terminus. Both stop on Fung Yip Street (Kailey Industrial Centre).
From Heng Fa Chuen
Bus 62 / 62A to Sun Yip Street, then walk over.
