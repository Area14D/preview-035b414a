09 · FAQ

Good to know.

How do I get in? 點樣入場？

The studio uses a code lock. You receive your entry code after the booking is confirmed. 確認後會收到門鎖密碼。

Can I bring in a large set? 可唔可以搬大型道具？

Yes. Cargo lift door 158 cm wide, 201 cm high; interior 259 × 225 cm. 可以。貨𨋢門闊 158 cm、高 201 cm。

When is the sun strongest? 幾時陽光最強？

The windows face east, so direct sun comes in during the morning on clear days. 窗向東，天晴嘅朝早最多直射陽光。

How dark is blackout? 全黑有幾黑？

With all blackout curtains closed the room is about 99% dark, even at midday. 全部遮光簾拉埋，中午都有大約 99% 黑。

Can I pay by company invoice? 可唔可以開公司發票？

Yes. Invoices are issued by Kinorrism Film Studio Co Ltd. 可以，由主義映畫有限公司發出。
